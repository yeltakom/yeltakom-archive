Original, Turkish

bi_özet okurları için öneri listeleri paylaştığımız “evdeyiz” serisinin yeni bölümünde, çalışmalarını mimarlık, sanat ve araştırma arakesitinde sürdüren Yelta Köm dijital eğitim platformlarından bir seçkiyi herkesin keyifli bir zamanında takip etmesi dileğiyle paylaşıyor.

Bugünlerde tüm dünyayı saran pandemi sebebiyle, evde çalışma ve durma ayrıcalığına sahip olanlar evlerinde kalıyor. Bununla beraber de tabii, özellikle son yıllarda gittikçe artan “aman iki dakika boş kalma, bir meşgalen olsun” söylemleri, hayat boyu eğitim ve sürekli gelişim baskısı altındayız. İki dakika rahatlayıp “faydalı” bir şey yapmayınca kendimizi kötü hissettiren bir atmosfer var. Normal zamanlarımızda, gündelik rutinimizde halihazırda yapmadığımız şeyleri, bu zamanda yapmadığımız için pişman hissetmenin bence kimseye de faydası yok.

Öte yandan gündelik alışkanlıklarımızı dijital platformlara taşırken yeni ortamlara giriyoruz, yeni deneyimler yaşıyoruz. Eğitim de bunun parçalarından biri: Dijital stüdyolar, jüriler, dersler; mimarlığın, tasarımın tartışılabildiği, farklılaştığı alanlara dönüşüyor. Şüphesiz dijital eğitim platformları yeni değil, uzun zamandır etrafımızdalar. Ben de takip ettiklerimden birkaç tanesini paylaşacağım, herkesin keyfi olduğunda takip etmesi dileğiyle. Bu öneriler sadece bu zamanlar için değil, başka zamanlar için de kenarda tutulup bakılabilir olmaları açısından da bir aciliyet ya da telaş gerektirmeyenlerden.

AI translation from Turkish, not reviewed by the author

bi_özet readers, in the new instalment of the "evdeyiz" (we are at home) series, in which we share recommendation lists, Yelta Köm, whose work continues at the intersection of architecture, art and research, shares a selection of digital education platforms, hoping that everyone follows them at a time that suits them.

Because of the pandemic that has gripped the whole world these days, those with the privilege of working and staying at home are staying at home. Along with that, of course, we are under the pressure of lifelong education and constant self-improvement, and of the "don't sit idle for even two minutes, keep yourself occupied" rhetoric that has been growing especially in recent years. There is an atmosphere that makes us feel bad if we do not take two minutes to relax and do something "useful". I think it is of no benefit to anyone to feel regret in this period for things we were not already doing in our normal times, in our daily routines.

On the other hand, as we move our daily habits onto digital platforms, we enter new environments and have new experiences. Education is one part of this: digital studios, juries and lectures are turning into spaces where architecture and design can be discussed and can diversify. Digital education platforms are certainly not new, they have been around us for a long time. I will share a few of the ones I follow, hoping that everyone follows them when it suits them. These recommendations are also ones that need no urgency or haste, so that they can be kept aside and looked at not only for these times but for other times too.

Vardiya Online

The Vardiya project (Vardiya means "shift"), which I was part of at the Venice Architecture Biennale two years ago, built up a substantial video archive. Both the conferences and the short presentations of professionals from different fields can be accessed online on Vardiya's YouTube channel.

Courses from MoMA on Coursera

Coursera is a very broad digital education platform, and it is possible to reach dozens of courses at different levels. In the course shaped by MoMA, professionals explain contemporary art through examples.

Modern Art & Ideas

What is Contemporary Art?

Courses from Various Universities on edX

edX is an education platform like Coursera. It is possible to take courses from different universities, and there are especially very good theory courses. The Architectural Imagination, offered by Harvard GSD, was quite popular for a while. The courses are not fully active at the moment, but it is possible to reach their recorded notes and videos.

YouTube Channels of Architecture and Design Schools

GSAPP YouTube

On the Columbia GSAPP YouTube channel I especially follow the conference broadcasts. The recording of the Digital Urbanisms conference from the past months is still up, and it can be interesting to look at the topics discussed there these days.

Städelschule Architecture Class (SAC) YouTube

Städelschule Architecture Class also publishes the presentations of invited guests at the school on its own channel. These presentations, especially in niche and little-explored areas, open the mind.
